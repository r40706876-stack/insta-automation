# Viewer review for MinuteMe GS visual experiments

Do not call a reel 10/10 or predict shares/retention from a successful render.
Before presenting a final preview, watch/inspect the exported video, with the same WhatsApp panel and layout used for publication.

Record:
- Who this specific reel is for, and why its first image/question matters to them.
- The single idea a viewer can explain after watching.
- What the animation demonstrates that the narration alone does not.
- What new information each scene adds; reject repeated scenes with only changed headings.
- A concrete reason someone might send it to a specific person. A generic share CTA is insufficient.
- Why the next episode would be worth following for.
- Whether the final WhatsApp/notes panel is readable outside interface overlays and preserves teaching space.
- Factual source and whether the scene simplifies or exaggerates a process.

Acknowledge weak or unsupported areas. A score is editorial opinion, not audience evidence.
A rejected export stays on publication hold. Preserve regular 7 PYQ and MEGA scheduling; do not silently substitute rejected visuals.
Compare actual audience results at matched ages before selecting a winning format.
