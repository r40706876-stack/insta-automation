# Reference-informed GS documentary experiment

Reference supplied by the user: 160.937s vertical history film. Inspected a full-duration 1-second contact sequence plus larger 3s/8s scene samples. The audio has not been independently transcribed; observations concern visible staging, on-screen words and sequencing.

What the reference does well: geographic establishing shot; camera moves motivated by places; route traces; historical objects/portraits paired with an event; date and person labels; recap, exam traps and a reusable final revision reference. The reference is not evidence of audience performance.

What this film changes: one self-contained question (why Meerut rebels went to Delhi), six narrative beats, no long logo introduction, three consecutive dates and the distinction between a symbolic leader and field command. Narration is newly written; no reference footage, music, branding or script is reused.

Production: real Blender 4.2 Eevee animated iron links, a sandstone fort model with a moving camera, NASA physical-geography raster, correct geographic city positions with explicitly schematic travel route, Cloudflare historical atmosphere illustration, Gemini Charon per-scene voice (existing female fallback only when unavailable), safe-area WhatsApp notes banner. Models are selected for a specific job; adding more models alone is not a quality gain.

The working portable Blender installation / EGL and Eevee compatibility approach was checked against r40706876-stack/Animation-special-; that separate project was read only. No octopus or wasp assets are repurposed into a history lesson.

Fact checks: CBSE social-science PDF documents 9 May punishment, 10 May Meerut revolt/release of comrades and 11 May arrival in Delhi. PIB confirms 85 sepoys and Meerut-to-Delhi movement. IIT Kanpur SATHEE describes Zafar as symbolic leader. Do not imply that one cartridge was the only cause, that all regions joined, or that the fort model is exact historical architecture.

Audience hypothesis: an exam partner can retell the three-day chain and explain Zafar's role. Shares and watch time must be measured after testing; no 10/10 or retention guarantee.

This workflow builds a preview. Publishing requires checking the actual export and a separate publication action; it does not change regular posting slots or resume current affairs.
